Piano Moving in Szentendre

Moving pianos, upright pianos and other large instruments from Budapest to Szentendre and back, with the Pest County +6,000 HUF surcharge.

Piano and upright piano moving in Szentendre

We move pianos, upright pianos and other large instruments from Budapest to Szentendre, from Szentendre to Budapest, and within Szentendre. Szentendre is in Pest County, so the surcharge is +6,000 HUF; we do not charge a per-kilometre fee.

How much does moving between Budapest and Szentendre cost?

  • Upright piano, ground floor: 39,000 + 6,000 = 45,000 HUF
  • Upright piano, 2 floors surcharge: 39,000 + 6,000 + 2 × 5,000 = 55,000 HUF
  • Grand piano, ground floor: 59,000 + 6,000 = 65,000 HUF
  • Grand piano, 2 floors surcharge: 59,000 + 6,000 + 2 × 9,000 = 83,000 HUF

Prices are indicative and may vary with the site. The full price list is on the prices page.

Typical locations

In the town centre there may be narrow streets and old houses, and steep driveways in the hillier parts. Please tell us in advance and, if possible, send a photo of the site.

What to include in your quote request

  • The instrument type, the pick-up and delivery address
  • The number of floors, and whether there is a lift
  • The preferred date (we work weekends and public holidays too)
  • If possible, a few photos of the site

Other towns and routes

For other towns in the area, see piano moving in Pest County; for moves within Budapest, see piano moving in Budapest; for longer trips, see nationwide piano moving.
