Piano Moving in Pest County

Moving pianos, upright pianos and other large instruments from Budapest to the surrounding towns and back, within Pest County with a +6,000 HUF surcharge.

Piano and upright piano moving in Pest County

We move pianos, upright pianos and other large instruments from Budapest to the surrounding towns, from there to Budapest, or within Pest County. Outside Budapest but within Pest County, the surcharge is +6,000 HUF; we only charge a per-kilometre fee on trips outside Pest County.

Which towns do we serve?

Without being exhaustive, frequent destinations are:

  • Buda agglomeration: Budaörs, Törökbálint, Biatorbágy, Budakeszi, Üröm, Diósd, Érd
  • Northern agglomeration: Szentendre, Dunakeszi, Gödöllő, Pécel
  • Southern and eastern agglomeration: Szigetszentmiklós, Vecsés, Gyál

If your town is not on the list but is in Pest County, we serve it just the same; feel free to call.

How much does moving cost in Pest County?

  • Upright piano: from 39,000 HUF + 6,000 HUF (Pest County surcharge) + 5,000 HUF per floor
  • Grand piano: from 59,000 HUF + 6,000 HUF (Pest County surcharge) + 9,000 HUF per floor

Example: an upright piano from ground floor to ground floor from Budapest to Budaörs: 39,000 + 6,000 = 45,000 HUF. Prices are indicative and may vary with the site. The full price list is on the prices page.

Family houses, gardens, stairs

Common locations in the surrounding towns are family houses where the entrance may be reached by garden steps, a gravel driveway or a narrow hallway. Please tell us in advance and, if possible, send a photo of the site.

What to include in your quote request

  • The instrument type, pick-up and delivery address, town
  • The number of floors and whether there is a lift
  • The preferred date (we work weekends and public holidays too)
