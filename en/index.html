Piano Moving Company in Budapest, Hungary

Öreg & Incze moves pianos, upright pianos and other large instruments in Budapest and across Hungary. We are a third-generation family business and have been doing this since 1995, seven days a week, with insurance and professional securing.

Price List

Who we are

We are a small family company based in Budapest. The work has been passed down in the family, and we handle every instrument as if it were our own. Our company holds general liability insurance, which covers damage that may occur during the move.

What we move

  • Upright pianos: from 39,000 HUF, see upright piano moving.
  • Grand pianos: from 59,000 HUF, including concert grands.
  • Harpsichords and other large instruments: quoted individually, see instrument moving.

How to ask for a quote

Call +36 20 962 9001 and tell us where the instrument is now, where it is going, the floors on both sides and whether there is a lift. A photo is not required; it is enough to tell us the details, and we will give you a price on the phone.

Where we work

  • Piano moving in Budapest: all 23 districts, upper floors included.
  • Pest County: towns around the capital for a flat surcharge of 6,000 HUF. We have town pages for Budaörs, Érd, Szentendre and Gödöllő.
  • Piano transport across Hungary: 250 HUF per kilometre outside Pest County.

Moving a piano in Budapest: a quick overview

Pianos usually travel on a stair-climbing trolley, wrapped and strapped, and the surcharge depends on the number of floors: 5,000 HUF per floor for an upright and 9,000 HUF per floor for a grand. We work on weekends and public holidays too, so you can pick the day that suits you.
